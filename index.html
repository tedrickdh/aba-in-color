ABOUT US

A community where connection has purpose.

ABA in Color was created to build stronger connections among professionals across Applied Behavior Analysis.

We create space for networking, collaboration, professional growth, leadership development, resource sharing, and authentic community.

Our community centers the experiences and advancement of professionals of color while welcoming everyone who supports the mission.

OUR MISSION Advancing equity through diverse leadership.
