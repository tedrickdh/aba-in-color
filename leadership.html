LEADERSHIP

Leadership should look like the community.

ABA in Color is building a leadership structure centered on service, accountability, representation, professional growth, and community impact.

HOW WE LEAD

Leadership is service.

We are not building titles. We are building responsibility.

Leadership in ABA in Color means helping create opportunities for others, protecting the mission, building trust, and moving the organization forward.

Our leaders are expected to serve with transparency, integrity, accountability, collaboration, and respect.

FOUNDING BOARD

Building the foundation.

The founding board is responsible for helping establish the organization, governance structure, priorities, and long-term direction of ABA in Color.

PRESIDENT

President

Provides strategic leadership, supports the board, helps guide organizational priorities, and represents ABA in Color publicly.

VICE PRESIDENT

Vice President

LEADERSHIP PRINCIPLES

What we expect from leaders.

01

Serve First

Leadership exists to support the mission and the people we serve.

02

Be Accountable

Leaders should follow through, communicate, and own their responsibilities.

03

Make Space

Strong leaders create opportunities for others to contribute and grow.

04

Protect the Mission

Decisions should support the purpose, values, and long-term health of the organization.

05

Build Trust

Transparency, respect, consistency, and ethical decision-making matter.

06

Develop Others

Leadership should create more leaders, not protect access to leadership.

COMMITTEES

There are many ways to lead.

Not every leadership role requires serving on the board. Committees allow members of the community to contribute expertise and help move specific priorities forward.

Events & Community

Help plan networking, professional, social, and community experiences.

Professional Development

Support workshops, speakers, mentorship, and learning opportunities.

Partnerships & Sponsorships

Build relationships with employers, universities, businesses, and community partners.

Membership & Community Engagement

Help strengthen connection, participation, communication, and community experience.

Communications

Support social media, storytelling, outreach, member spotlights, and public awareness.

Governance & Operations

Support policies, organizational systems, records, planning, and nonprofit operations.

GOVERNANCE

Build it right. Grow it responsibly.

ABA in Color is building the structure needed to operate responsibly, including board governance, bylaws, financial oversight, organizational policies, meeting procedures, and nonprofit compliance.

As the organization grows, governance information, public reports, and key organizational documents can be made available here.

THE LEADERSHIP PIPELINE

Today's participant can become tomorrow's leader.

We want ABA in Color to create pathways for people to move from attending an event, to volunteering, to serving on a committee, to leading programs, and eventually serving in formal organizational leadership.
