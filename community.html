MORE THAN NETWORKING

Professional community should feel personal.

Networking can open doors. Community helps people walk through them.

ABA in Color is designed to create meaningful professional and social connection across the field of Applied Behavior Analysis.

We want people to have a place to share ideas, ask questions, learn from each other, build relationships, celebrate wins, find opportunities, and navigate challenges with people who understand the work.

COME AS YOU ARE.

BELONGING & PSYCHOLOGICAL SAFETY

You shouldn't have to shrink to fit the room.

Professional spaces work better when people feel respected, heard, and comfortable enough to contribute honestly.

ABA in Color is building spaces where people can connect across professional roles while still showing up authentically.

That means creating room for conversation, support, collaboration, different perspectives, shared experiences, and growth.
