WHY WE EXIST

More than a seat at the table.

We're building the relationships, visibility, leadership pipeline, and professional community needed to help shape the future of ABA.

ABA in Color creates space for professionals to connect authentically, share resources, develop professionally, find opportunities, build leadership skills, and support one another.

WHAT WE'RE BUILDING

Five pillars. One community.

01

Community

Build authentic professional and social connections in spaces where people feel welcomed, supported, and valued.

02

Leadership

Develop professionals who are prepared to lead teams, organizations, businesses, and the future of the field.

03

Opportunity

Expand access to jobs, mentorship, speaking opportunities, partnerships, and professional visibility.

04

Development

Create opportunities for learning, professional growth, mentorship, collaboration, and skill development.

05

Impact

Turn relationships and ideas into meaningful action for professionals, organizations, and communities.
