ABOUT ABA IN COLOR

Community with purpose.

ABA in Color was created to build connection, increase professional opportunity, strengthen leadership, and create space where diverse voices across Applied Behavior Analysis can thrive.

OUR STORY

It started with a need for connection.

ABA professionals deserve spaces where professional growth and authentic community can exist together.

ABA in Color began in Houston with a simple goal: create stronger connections among professionals of color working throughout Applied Behavior Analysis.

That vision has grown into something larger: a community focused on leadership, professional development, collaboration, opportunity, visibility, and impact.

While ABA in Color centers the experiences and advancement of professionals of color, everyone who supports the mission is welcome.

OUR MISSION

Advance equity through diverse leadership.

We create opportunities for ABA professionals to be seen, supported, connected, developed, and positioned to lead.

OUR VISION

A field where talent has access.

We envision a profession where professionals from every background have meaningful access to mentorship, leadership, opportunity, community, and professional growth.
